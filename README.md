# 🌍 Travel Planner – Welcome to the world of wonders!
Are you tired of C programs that just calculate grades and add two numbers?  
Let’s go for a journey into the woods!

*Travel Planner* is a quirky, beginner-friendly terminal-based C program that lets users explore dream destinations across all seven continents tailored to their vibe and climate preference.

No graphics. No internet. Just pure C code and a world of imagination.

## ✨ Features You’ll Love

Here’s what makes this travel planner more than just another C project:

# 1. *Continent Explorer*
Pick from:
- Asia
- Europe
- North America
- South America
- Africa
- Australia
- Antarctica (yes, the penguin place)

# 2. *Country Selector*
Each continent gives you 4 handpicked countries (Antarctica has just one—obviously). No need to Google.

# 3. *Climate Preference*
Are you a beach bum or a snow seeker? Choose your comfort:
- Hot – Tropical getaways, deserts, and beach cities
- Cold – Mountains, highlands, or even the South Pole

# 4. *Instant Destination Ideas*
Based on your input, the planner recommends 2 iconic places per country that match your selected climate.  

# 5. *Error Handling*
Accidentally type “9” for continent? No worries. This one politely tells you to stay within bounds.  
We don’t send you to imaginary planets. Yet.


# 🧠 Who Is This For?

- *C Programming Students*: Learn conditionals, switch cases, input validation, and logical structuring—fun style.
- *Beginners Building Projects*: Replace boring textbook exercises with something you can proudly show off.
- *Travel Lovers*: Get random wanderlust delivered straight to your terminal.
- *Educators*: Use this as a creative assignment to introduce decision-based logic.
